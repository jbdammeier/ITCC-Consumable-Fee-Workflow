About This Workflow

A. Purpose

This workflow replaces the previous DocuSign-based consumable fee change process with a Power Automate flow built entirely on Microsoft 365 tools already licensed by Ivy Tech Community College. The previous process presented several challenges: limited flexibility in form design, no built-in mechanism to send requests back for revision, difficulty tracking where a request stood at any given point, inconsistent storage and retrieval of finalized approval documents, and limited visibility and transparency for all parties involved. Data accuracy was also difficult to verify, as the process relied on manual entry across disconnected steps.

This flow addresses those challenges by centralizing submission, routing, approvals, revision tracking, and document generation in a single connected system. Approvers receive consistent notifications with the supporting document attached at every step. The status of any request is visible at any time through the SharePoint list and available for reporting. Campus-based fee distribution, including through the bookstore, is included in scope.

Because this flow is built on Microsoft SharePoint, Power Automate, Microsoft Forms, and Microsoft Teams, which are part of Ivy Tech's existing licensing and are not likely to be replaced in the near future, it offers long-term sustainability. The flow is designed to require minimal ongoing maintenance once built, supported by documentation and a testing protocol managed by the process designer.

B. Scope
Included in This Flow
  • New fee submissions
  • Fee increase requests
  • Fee decrease requests
  • Fee removal requests
  • Annual SharePoint list setup and population
  • Document generation and storage
  • Reporting data (Power BI and Finance exports)
Out of Scope / Handled Separately
  • No-Change records (pre-loaded, no approval flow triggered)
  • Fee implementation in the student information system
  • External vendor contract management

Stage 2 : Approval Routing
Timeout and Rejection Rules — apply to every approval step in Stage 2: Approvers have 48–72 business hours to respond. No response triggers one reminder, then the submission is treated as "Request Changes" and returned to the Chair. A hard rejection cancels the flow immediately — Chair and SOL are notified with the rejection reason, and the record is marked "Rejected" in SharePoint. See the Systems Office Liaison box below for the full response pattern.

Items for Reviewer Input

The following items require decisions before the flow can be fully built. Items are grouped by topic. Dashed-border steps in the flow above correspond to decisions marked "Needs Your Input."

Approval Chain
D1
Needs Your Input
Testing Center Review Step
Several fees are distributed through the Testing Center. Should the Executive Director Testing Services have a conditional review step? If so, should it be a formal approval or a notification only?
D2
Please Confirm
Finance Formal Step
Finance currently reviews final data outside the flow. The current plan does not include a Finance approval step. Reviewers should confirm this is acceptable or decide if adding a step would improve accuracy.
D3
Needs Your Input
OIT Review Step
Some fees are fulfilled through OIT Shared Services Software such as Adobe. Should OIT have a conditional review step when the distributor is OIT, similar to how eCampus is handled?
D4
Needs Your Input
Central Account Process
Several fees route to a Central Account administrator. Should this be a notification step, a formal approval, or handled entirely outside the flow?
Form and Data
D5
Needs Your Input
Item Vendor Field Format
Should Item Vendor be a free-text field or a managed dropdown? A dropdown reduces errors but requires maintenance. Free text is simpler but may lead to inconsistencies across submissions.
D6
Confirmed
Approver Entry Method
Confirmed: the Chair enters approver contact information on the form in the current version. A future upgrade will pull this automatically from a committee lookup table.
Special Cases
D7
Needs Your Input
High-Dollar or Third-Party Fees
Some third-party vendor fees reach very high amounts, such as flight training programs above $30,000. Should fees above a defined dollar threshold trigger an additional review step or a different handling process?
Documents and Records
D8
In Progress
Document Template Design
The Word document template for generated approval records is being designed based on an existing version. Must include fee details, DoE scenario justifications, approval information, and metadata. Owner and target completion date to be confirmed before external review.
D9
Needs Your Input
Draft Version Retention
If a submission goes through multiple revision cycles, should each draft document version be kept in the Document Library, or only the final approved version?
D10
Needs Your Input
Who Can Issue a Hard Reject
A hard reject cancels the flow immediately and cannot be revised or resubmitted without starting over. Should this option be available to all approvers at every step, or should it be restricted to specific roles such as the Provost or SOL only?
